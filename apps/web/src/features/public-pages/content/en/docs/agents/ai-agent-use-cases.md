# What Can AI Agents Do for You?

> AI agents are highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work

See [What are AI agents](/docs/agents/what-are-ai-agents)

Below are some ways AI agents can work for you.

## 1. Find and Apply for Opportunities You Would Otherwise Miss

Finding the right job, scholarship, grant, or business opportunity can take hours to months of searching across different websites.

Your agent can browse relevant websites, investigate opportunities, compare requirements, organize the results, help prepare applications, submit applications and respond to invitations while keeping you informed.

- Find and apply for "best advantage" jobs for you.
- Find and apply for scholarships.
- Find and apply for funding/grants for which you may be eligible.

**Example prompt**

> Find fully funded master's scholarships in the UK for international students. Search university websites, scholarship databases, and other relevant sources. Compare eligibility requirements and prepare a calendar of upcoming deadlines. Maintain a ranking based on rating of the school and my eligibility. Do an exhaustive search over the next 72 hours. Update me as you work. Send me a brief every morning at 8 am UK time, including new opportunities, deadlines, and source links.

## 2. Find/Compare/Monitor the Best Deals and Save Money

Instead of visiting dozens of websites yourself, ask your agent to search, compare, and monitor offers according to your requirements.

- Find discounts and deals on products, property, land you're interested in.
- Compare flights, phones, laptops, cars, and other products.
- Monitor prices and look for better offers.

**Example prompt**

> Find return flights from Berlin to New York for a 7–10 day trip in March. My budget is €450 per person. Compare airlines, flexible dates, nearby airports, baggage allowances, and booking conditions. Search for the best available deals over the next 14 days. Rank your results. Update me as you work. Send me a daily summary at 9 am Berlin time. Alert me if you find a suitable flight below €350.

## 3. Grow Your Business and Find New Customers

Your agent can research your market, browse company websites, identify promising prospects, and help prepare your outreach.

- Find and email potential customers that match your target market.
- Research companies and their products, services, and business needs.
- Identify potential partners, suppliers, and distributors.
- Monitor competitors and market developments.
- Build lead lists and draft and send personalized outreach messages.

**Example prompt**

> Find 100 small and medium-sized businesses in Germany that could benefit from our AI video translation service. Research their websites to identify businesses publishing videos for international audiences. For each prospect, provide its website, why it is a good fit, and publicly available business contact details. Rank the leads by relevance and prepare a personalized outreach email for each. Work on this over the next 7 days and send me a progress report every morning at 8 am Berlin time.

## 4. Plan Your Life, Travel, and Everyday Activities

From planning a family holiday to researching a major purchase, your agent can gather information from multiple sources and turn it into a practical plan.

- Plan holidays around your budget, interests, and dates.
- Compare accommodation, transport, and activities.
- Find family-friendly attractions and free things to do.
- Research important decisions and compare available options.
- Organize tasks, deadlines, and reminders.

**Example prompt**

> Plan a 5-day family holiday in Italy for 2 adults and 3 teenagers, with a total budget of €1,500. Research travel options, accommodation, and attractions using relevant websites. Include free activities, estimated costs, and a day-by-day itinerary. I want an exhaustive research. The holiday is 3 months away. Look for better deals twice each day and update the plan when you find meaningful savings.

## 5. Monitor, Research, and Report Back

Some tasks require more than a one-time search. Your agent can help investigate a topic, interact with websites, gather information, and track changes over time.

- Monitor websites for new listings, announcements, and price changes.
- Research companies, people, markets, and topics.
- Analyze expenditure, accounting data, or information you provide.
- Track competitors and summarize important developments.
- Prepare recurring reports and useful briefings.

**Example prompt**

> Monitor the websites of our five main competitors for new products, pricing changes, major announcements, and marketing campaigns. Check every weekday, summarize the important changes, and send me a concise report at 8 am Berlin time. Include source links and explain why each change might matter to our business.
