# Jupiter

Jupiter is the leading DEX aggregator on Solana. It scans all major Solana decentralized exchanges to find you the best price for any token swap — no account setup needed.

## What it's good for

- **Best prices** — By aggregating liquidity across multiple sources, Jupiter usually gives you a better rate than using any single exchange.
- **Fast swaps** — Transactions settle in seconds on Solana's fast blockchain.
- **No KYC** — Connect your Solana wallet and swap tokens instantly. No email or ID required.

## How it works

1. Connect your Solana wallet (like Phantom or Backpack).
2. Tell your agent which tokens to swap and how much.
3. Jupiter finds the best route across multiple DEXs and executes the trade on your behalf.

## Fees

- Trading fees depend on the specific DEX route Jupiter chooses. Jupiter itself does not charge an extra fee for the aggregation service.
- Solana network (gas) fees are very low — typically fractions of a cent per transaction.

## Best use case

Pick Jupiter if you trade tokens on Solana and want fast, cheap swaps at the best available price. It's the go-to venue for everyday token trading in the Solana ecosystem.

## Limitations

- **Solana only** — Jupiter works exclusively on the Solana blockchain. If you trade on Ethereum or other chains, use 1inch instead.
- **Spot trading only** — Jupiter does not support leverage or perpetual futures. For leveraged trading, use Hyperliquid.
