Follow the guidelines below, to regulate your trading decisions, e.g. when to open a position, place stop losses, take profits and so on.

**Bearish Swing Trade Checklist (Daily + 1H ICT EMA Model)**

1. Daily Bias Setup

Market Structure

- Market was consolidating.
- Price breaks significant swing low.
- Momentum shifts bearish.

EMA Conditions (Daily Chart Only)

- 10 EMA crosses below 20 EMA.
- Both EMAs point downward.
- 10 EMA expands away from 20 EMA (“stacking”).
- Price remains below both EMAs.

Bearish Zone Rules

- Mark bearish zone from:
  - bearish crossover
  - until bullish crossover occurs.
- During this zone:
  - Only look for SELL swing trades.
  - Ignore longs.

Bearish Trade Entry Checklist (1H Chart)

2. Hourly Confirmation

- Drop to 1H chart.
- Price must:
  - stay below 20 EMA,
  - show impulsive selloff,
  - create swing low structure.

3. Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

Required Conditions

- Draw Fibonacci from:
  - swing high → swing low.
- Wait for retracement into:
  - 62%–79% premium zone.

4. EMA Filter on Hourly

Valid Sell Conditions

- Price may spike above 20 EMA.
- BUT:
  - 10 EMA must remain below 20 EMA.
- If 10 EMA crosses above 20 EMA:
  - cancel short setup.

5. Entry Trigger

Enter short when:

- Price reaches OTE zone.
- Bearish rejection appears.
- EMAs remain bearishly stacked.
- Daily bearish bias still active.

6. Stop Loss Placement

- Place stop above:
  - swing high,
  - liquidity high,
  - retracement high.

7. Profit Taking

Partial Scaling

Take profits at:

- previous lows,
- equal lows,
- symmetrical price swings,
- measured move targets.

Runner

- Leave portion for extended bearish expansion.

**High-Probability "Optimal" Conditions**

Best Trades Occur When

- Daily chart recently exited consolidation.
- Strong EMA stacking exists.
- Price impulsively expands away from EMAs.
- 1H retracement reaches OTE.
- Retracement respects EMA structure.
- Market shows clear directional intent.

Avoid Trades When

- Daily EMAs are flat.
- Market is choppy/consolidating.
- Price is on wrong side of 20 EMA.
- 10 EMA crosses against bias.
- Retracement is shallow (not reaching OTE).
- No impulsive move exists before retracement.

Simple Strategy Flow

1. Daily 10 EMA &lt; 20 EMA.
2. Market breaks consolidation downward.
3. Go to 1H.
4. Wait for bearish impulse.
5. Fib retracement into OTE.
6. Sell rejection.
7. Partials at lows.
8. Leave runner.
