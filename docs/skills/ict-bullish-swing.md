During a bullish market, follow the guidelines below, to regulate your trading decisions, e.g. when to open a position, place stop losses, take profits and so on.

**Bullish Swing Trade Checklist (Daily + 1H ICT EMA Model)**

1. Daily Bias Setup (Must Be Present First)

Market Structure

- Market was previously in consolidation.
- Price breaks a significant swing high.
- Daily momentum shifts bullish.

EMA Conditions (Daily Chart Only)

- 10 EMA crosses above 20 EMA.
- Both EMAs are pointing upward.
- 10 EMA is expanding away from the 20 EMA (“stacking”).
- Price remains above both EMAs.

Bullish Zone Rules

- Mark the bullish period from:
  - the 10 EMA crossover above the 20 EMA
  - until the 10 EMA crosses back below the 20 EMA.
- During this bullish zone:
  - Only look for BUY swing trades.
  - Ignore shorts.

Bullish Trade Entry Checklist (1H Chart)

2. Hourly Confirmation

- Drop to the 1H chart.
- Price must:
  - be above the 20 EMA,
  - show impulsive bullish movement,
  - create a swing high.

3. Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

Required Conditions

- Draw Fibonacci from:
  - swing low → swing high.
- Wait for retracement into:
  - 62%–79% OTE zone.
- Retracement should tap into:
  - previous swing low area,
  -	imbalance,
  -	body support,
  -	or discount zone.
- EMA Filter on Hourly

Valid Buy Conditions

- Price may briefly dip below the 20 EMA.
- BUT:
  -	the 10 EMA must NOT cross below the 20 EMA.
- If 10 EMA crosses below 20 EMA:
  -	cancel long setup.

5. Entry Trigger

Enter long when:

- Price reaches OTE zone.
- Bullish rejection appears.
- EMAs remain bullish stacked.
- Higher timeframe bullish bias still active.

6. Stop Loss Placement

- Place stop below:
  - swing low,
  -	liquidity low,
  -	or retracement low.
- Do NOT aggressively trail early.

7. Profit Taking

Partial Scaling

- Take first partial at:
  -	prior highs,
  -	equal highs,
  -	or symmetrical price swing.

Runner

- Leave small position open for:
  -	daily expansion,
  -	continuation trend,
  -	200%+ extension.

8. Trade Management Rules

During Consolidation

- Keep stop wider.
- Avoid tight trailing.

After Expansion

- Trail behind higher swing lows.

**High-Probability "Optimal" Conditions**

Best Trades Occur When

- Daily chart recently exited consolidation.
- Strong EMA stacking exists.
- Price impulsively expands away from EMAs.
- 1H retracement reaches OTE.
- Retracement respects EMA structure.
- Market shows clear directional intent.

Avoid Trades When

- Daily EMAs are flat.
- Market is choppy/consolidating.
- Price is on wrong side of 20 EMA.
- 10 EMA crosses against bias.
- Retracement is shallow (not reaching OTE).
- No impulsive move exists before retracement.

Simple Strategy Flow

1.	Daily 10 EMA > 20 EMA.
2.	Market breaks consolidation upward.
3.	Go to 1H.
4.	Wait for bullish impulse.
5.	Fib retracement into OTE.
6.	Buy rejection.
7.	Partials at highs.
8.	Leave runner.
